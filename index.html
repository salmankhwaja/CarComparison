How the numbers are worked out
  • True monthly cost is the fixed amount a month whose invested value at the end of the period equals the invested value of every rupee the option takes, minus what you get back when you sell. It already includes the opportunity cost of your money.
  • Depreciation uses a constant yearly loss of value. Insurance is charged on the car's falling value.
  • EMI uses a standard reducing-balance loan. A loan still running at the end is paid off from the sale. Warnings flag the SBP auto-finance rules (Rs 30 lakh cap per person, at most 70% financed, 3 years above 1000cc, 5 years up to 1000cc, local EVs up to 7) as reported in late 2026. Confirm with your bank.
  • Withholding tax presets are for filers; non-filers pay about three times as much. Registration is a percentage of the price.
  • Long trips: petrol, hybrid and plug-in cars drive them; EVs and ride-hailing rent a car for each trip.
  • Running costs (fuel, power, fares, upkeep, driver) rise each year at your inflation rate. Amounts are in rupees as you'd pay them.

For education only, not financial advice. Prices in the presets are from public listings dated in data/presets.json; dealers, taxes and fuel prices change often.
